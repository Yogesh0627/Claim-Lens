# Product Overview

ClaimLens is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to help insurance companies manage, investigate, analyze, and process insurance claims efficiently while reducing fraud and improving operational transparency.

The platform combines claim management, document management, OCR processing, image analysis, fraud scoring, investigator workflow management, policy-driven automation, analytics, and auditability into a single unified system.

# Problem Statement

Insurance companies face several operational challenges during claim processing:

* Manual claim investigation processes
* Fraudulent and duplicate claims
* Large volumes of documents requiring verification
* Slow claim turnaround times
* Uneven investigator workload distribution
* Lack of centralized claim visibility
* Limited auditability and compliance tracking
* Inconsistent claim handling procedures across branches and regions

These challenges increase operational costs, delay claim settlements, and negatively impact customer experience.

# Multi-Tenant Strategy

ClaimLens is designed as a multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Each insurance company operates within its own isolated tenant environment while sharing the same platform infrastructure.

Benefits:

* Lower operational cost
* Centralized platform management
* Tenant isolation
* Faster onboarding
